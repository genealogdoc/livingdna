Creating Your Living DNA Login Account
--------------------------------------

Establishing your Living DNA login account is a straightforward process designed to get you on the path to discovery quickly and securely. To create your account, you will need a valid email address and a strong password. When choosing your password, it is advisable to use a combination of letters, numbers, and special characters to ensure the highest level of security for your personal data[citation:1].

The creation process is simple. You will be asked to read and agree to the terms and conditions to proceed. Once you have set up your email and password, Living DNA will send a validation email to the address you provided. This is a critical security step that confirms you have access to the email account and that your Living DNA login account is legitimate. You must click the link within this email to validate your address before you can fully access your account and begin activating any test kits[citation:1].

Activating Your DNA Kit
-----------------------

A common point of confusion for new users is that purchasing a test kit does not automatically create your Living DNA login account; you must do this separately[citation:5]. Once your account is set up and validated, you can proceed to activate your kit. To do this, log in to your account and click on the 'Activate' option. You will be prompted to enter the twelve-character activation code that came with your kit[citation:5].

It is important to enter this code carefully. Characters can sometimes be mistaken for one another, such as the letter 'B' and the number '8', or 'J' and '3'. The letters in your code must be capitalised, and you do not need to add any hyphens, as the system will do this automatically[citation:5]. During activation, you will also need to fill out the personal details of the person providing the DNA sample, including their full name, date of birth, and place of birth. This information is crucial for processing the sample and delivering accurate results[citation:5].

Troubleshooting Access to Your Account
--------------------------------------

Like many online services, you may occasionally encounter issues when trying to access your Living DNA login account. Fortunately, most common problems have simple solutions. One of the most frequent culprits is using the wrong email address. It is possible to purchase a kit with one email but register your portal with another. Double-checking which address you used for your Living DNA login account is a good first step[citation:2].

If you are sure your email is correct but cannot remember your password, you can easily reset it through the login page, provided you still have access to the email address associated with your account[citation:6]. However, if you see an error stating that your email or password is incorrect, a simple typing error could be the cause[citation:6].

For those who have set up two-factor authentication, which is mandatory for all Living DNA accounts, logging in requires an additional step. After entering your username and password, a unique six-digit code is sent to your registered email address. This provides an extra layer of security and ensures that only you can access your account, even if someone else knows your password. If you do not see the email with the code, be sure to check your spam or junk folder[citation:10].

Your web browser can also be a source of login difficulties. To get the best experience and avoid issues, Living DNA recommends using the latest version of Google Chrome, Safari, or Firefox[citation:2]. If you have been using a different browser and are having trouble, try switching to one of these recommended options. In some cases, your browser's cookies and cache can cause problems. Clearing them can often resolve performance issues with the website[citation:2].

Another common issue is ad-blocking software or internet security programs, such as Kaspersky, which can interfere with the site's functionality. If you are having trouble, try temporarily disabling this software to see if it resolves the problem. For Firefox users, the Enhanced Tracking Protection feature can sometimes cause issues, so you may need to disable it for the Living DNA site[citation:2].

Navigating the New Living DNA Portal
------------------------------------

After you have successfully logged in, you will be welcomed by the newly redesigned Living DNA portal. This modern and improved interface unites your Ancestry and Health profiles in a seamless experience, making it easier than ever to explore your DNA insights. The main menu, located across the top of the screen on desktops or accessible via a blue menu button on smaller devices, is your command centre[citation:7].

Your Dashboard provides a quick overview of your DNA results and activity. Here, you can see your profile information, check the status of any tests you have purchased, and find interactive summaries of your recent ancestry. The dashboard also features cards that display your maternal and paternal haplogroups, if you are genetically male, or just your maternal haplogroup if you are genetically female[citation:7].

From the main menu, you can explore different sections of your results. Under 'Ancestry', you can delve into your 'Recent Ancestry', which is presented on an interactive map that you can zoom in and scroll around. Your 'Motherline' and 'Fatherline' sections are also accessible here, providing a deep dive into your ancient migration history. For those who have purchased upgrades, this is also where you can explore your 'Viking', 'Neanderthal', or 'Classical' ancestry results[citation:7].

If you have taken a health test, your results are accessible under the 'Health' section of the menu. This includes both Genetic Health results and, for UK customers, any Blood Test results you have taken. The portal is designed to provide a comprehensive view of your genetic profile, all in one place[citation:7].

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

**Question: How do I create a Living DNA login account?**

You can create an account by visiting the Living DNA website and providing your email address and a secure password. You will need to agree to the terms and conditions and validate your email address by clicking a link sent to your inbox before you can fully access your account[citation:1].

**Question: Why can I not access my Living DNA login account?**

Common issues include using the wrong email address, forgetting your password, or browser-related problems. Try resetting your password, checking your spam folder for 2FA codes, clearing your browser's cache, or using a recommended browser like Chrome, Safari, or Firefox[citation:2][citation:6][citation:10].

**Question: How do I activate my DNA test kit with my Living DNA login account?**

Log in to your account, click 'Activate', and enter the twelve-character activation code from your kit. Ensure the letters are capitalised and you are using the correct email address you used for your Living DNA login account. Also, you must be using a compatible browser[citation:5].

**Question: Can I view a test for someone else in my Living DNA login account?**

The owner of the test has ultimate control over who can see their results. If you have been added as a manager, you will need your own Living DNA login account. If you cannot view a profile you manage, check that the correct email address is saved in the permissions section of the DNA profiles[citation:12].

**Question: What is the new Living DNA portal like?**

The Living DNA portal has been redesigned to unite ancestry and health results in a single, seamless experience. It features a modern interface with an interactive dashboard, easy-to-navigate menus, and detailed results presented in a more user-friendly and visually engaging way[citation:7].
